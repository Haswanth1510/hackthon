Pre-Scan Preparation Steps

Follow these 4 clinical guidelines before scanning

1
Balanced & Natural Lighting
Face natural window daylight or a well-lit room. Avoid harsh backlighting, strong colored LED lights, or deep side shadows.
2
Clean & Bare Skin (No Heavy Makeup)
Scan bare skin for accurate pore, barrier, and sebum telemetry. Remove eyeglasses, sunglasses, masks, or hats that occlude landmarks.
3
Eye-Level & Neutral Expression
Hold camera directly at eye level (30–45 cm away). Keep a relaxed, neutral facial expression looking straight ahead with mouth closed.
4
Human Face Alignment & Scan
Center face inside the viewport until the green 468-point mesh locks on, then tap Analyze My Skin (or upload a human photo).
